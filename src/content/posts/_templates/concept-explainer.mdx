## Short Answer

Two or three sentences maximum. Give the complete answer right here — no teasing,
no "keep reading to find out." A reader who stops here should leave informed.

---

## Why It Matters

Who is asking this and why does the answer change anything for them?
Ground the question in a real context — a decision someone has to make,
a misconception that costs them time or money, a concept that unlocks
a whole area of knowledge once understood.
