### Evidence 2: [Counter-evidence or nuance]

## Where the Myth Comes From

Trace the origin: who said it first, in what context, and how it spread.
Was it true once and became outdated? Was it a misquotation? Was it true
in a narrower context and then overgeneralized?

This section should make the reader understand why smart people believe this —
not mock them for believing it.

---

## What's Actually True

The accurate, nuanced version of the claim. This is not just "the opposite" —
it is the precise, sourced statement that replaces the myth.

If there is genuine uncertainty in the evidence, say so explicitly. Distinguish
between "we don't know" and "this is false."
